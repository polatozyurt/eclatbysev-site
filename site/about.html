Our Story

About Éclat By Sev

Éclat By Sev was created for women who want elegance to feel effortless. It is a brand built on the belief that jewellery should not only complete a look, but transform how you feel in it.

After years of working within one of Turkey's leading luxury fashion houses, I learned something simple but important: looking refined should not require spending a fortune — but it should never mean settling for less.

That is why I created Éclat: where Parisian elegance meets the craftsmanship of Istanbul.

We create refined, statement pieces designed to elevate even the simplest outfit — with intention, softness, and lasting presence.

Founded by Sev

Éclat By Sev is a founder-led jewellery label shaped by a feminine, modern, and detail-focused point of view. Every collection begins with the same intention: to create pieces that feel elevated, wearable, and emotionally expressive.

Craftsmanship

Our pieces are developed with a strong focus on quality, balance, and finish. We believe great jewellery should feel special from the moment you put it on — and remain a piece you reach for again and again.

Small-Batch Philosophy

We prefer thoughtful, limited production over excess. By working in smaller runs, we stay closer to the design process, reduce unnecessary waste, and create collections that feel more intentional.

The Meaning of Éclat

The word Éclat means radiance. It reflects exactly how we want every woman to feel when she wears our pieces: confident, luminous, and effortlessly striking.

Handcrafted jewellery for the woman who shines. Designed by Sev, made in small batches.
